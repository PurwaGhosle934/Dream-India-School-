About Our School

Dream India School, located in Sai Priya Nagar, Rayagada, is committed to providing holistic education that balances academics with co-curricular activities.

We believe in creating a safe, stimulating, and supportive environment where every child is encouraged to explore their potential and develop a lifelong love for learning.

Our dedicated faculty and staff work tirelessly to ensure that students not only excel in their studies but also grow into responsible and compassionate citizens.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic and transformative education that nurtures intellectual curiosity, moral values, and physical well-being. We are committed to:

  • Delivering academic excellence through innovative teaching methods.
  • Creating a safe, inclusive, and stimulating learning environment.
  • Fostering critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills.
  • Instilling a sense of responsibility, discipline, and respect for others.

Our Vision

To emerge as a premier educational institution in Odisha, recognized globally for shaping future leaders and responsible citizens. We envision:

  • A school where every child discovers and realizes their true potential.
  • An environment that blends modern technology with traditional values.
  • Empowered students who are ready to face global challenges with confidence.
  • A community of lifelong learners and compassionate human beings.

Our Unique Academic Program

Dream India School designed a Unique Academic Program to meet the learning needs of different grades of students.

Pre-Primary School

Our Pre-Primary education is endowed with a distinct environment where all aspects of childhood personality are crafted in a balanced way, unleashing creativity and pre-primary academic excellence.

Primary School

Our Primary school education infuses within our students the desire to learn, the curiosity to know more, kindness towards society, and skills to lead in future.

Middle School

DIS has woven the approach of core experiential learning with technology. We begin to pass the envelope of IIT & Medicine foundation courses to provide a robust approach to qualitative learning.

High School

High School is the most critical juncture of career development. We focus on 'IIT & Medicine Foundation' classes to absorb conscience, capability, and credibility for future leaders.

Special Foundation Programs

Integrated IIT / Medicine Foundation

DIS pays special attention to students' exceptional flair for Math and Science. We offer an integrated IIT / Medicine foundation program for classes VI - X students to give the much-needed extra input for carving out a successful career.

This integrated foundation program is crafted and imparted with the regular curriculum to enrich the students' analytical skills and subject knowledge, helping them appear for competitive exams like IIT / Medicine confidently.

General Studies (VI - X) & GK (I - V)

We train our students, especially in General knowledge / General Studies to keep them updated with what is happening across the globe and to appear for all sorts of competitive exams across India like IIT / Medicine / UPSC etc. from an early age confidently.

Our Facilities

Computer Lab

Full-fledged computer lab to integrate technology in support of classroom learning, with Online Teaching Classes accessible even to remote areas.

Digital Classrooms

Equipped with Modern Audio-Visual devices and digital content. Acts as Dream Remote Teaching Room (DRTR) to learn from the best teachers globally.

English Language Lab

Embedded English software into computers, specially designed to overcome language challenges across heterogenic cultures.

Well Equipped Science Lab

Dedicated science labs to bridge theoretical lessons with practical exposure. All experiments are conducted under the safety and guidance of specialized faculty.

Meditation & Yoga

DIS conducts meditation in every session to help students improve concentration, find inner peace, reduce stress, and stay happy.

CCTV Surveillance

24/7 CCTV monitoring across the campus to ensure complete safety and security of our students.
